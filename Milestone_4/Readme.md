⚙️ Milestone 4 – Baseline Pricing Engine

The objective of this milestone is to build a rule-based pricing engine that adjusts product prices using predefined business rules.
Pricing rules were implemented based on time (peak periods) and inventory levels to simulate dynamic pricing behavior.
The system generates adjusted prices and compares them with original static prices.
Revenue is calculated for both scenarios, and revenue lift is measured to evaluate performance improvement.
This baseline model serves as a foundation for comparison with machine learning-based pricing in later stages.
