## Part 6: Risk Management

### Potential Risks & Mitigation

#### 1. Revenue Decrease Risk
**Risk**: Prices decrease too much, reducing revenue
**Probability**: Low (8% of cases in backtesting)
**Impact**: Revenue loss of 1-5%
**Mitigation**:
- [ ] Set minimum margin floors
- [ ] Implement price decrease caps
- [ ] Manual approval for big changes
- [ ] A/B testing before full deployment
- [ ] Daily monitoring with alerts

#### 2. Customer Dissatisfaction Risk
**Risk**: Customers unhappy with price volatility
**Probability**: Low (most see discounts)
**Impact**: Customer churn or negative reviews
**Mitigation**:
- [ ] Transparent communication about pricing
- [ ] Gradual price changes vs dramatic shifts
- [ ] Loyalty program integration
- [ ] Customer feedback channels
- [ ] PR plan for price changes

#### 3. Competitive Response Risk
**Risk**: Competitors match our price cuts, reducing advantage
**Probability**: Medium (likely in competitive markets)
**Impact**: Reduced revenue improvement
**Mitigation**:
- [ ] Monitor competitor responses
- [ ] Adjust strategy dynamically
- [ ] Focus on value beyond price
- [ ] Differentiation strategies
- [ ] Market share vs margin optimization

#### 4. System Technical Risk
**Risk**: System downtime, data errors, bugs
**Probability**: Low (<1% month)
**Impact**: Pricing errors, lost revenue
**Mitigation**:
- [ ] Redundant systems and backups
- [ ] Thorough testing before deployment
- [ ] 24/7 monitoring and alerts
- [ ] Quick rollback procedures
- [ ] Manual override options

#### 5. Adoption Risk
**Risk**: Store staff don't use system effectively
**Probability**: Low with training
**Impact**: Reduced revenue improvement
**Mitigation**:
- [ ] Comprehensive training program
- [ ] Easy-to-use dashboard
- [ ] Local champion approach
- [ ] Incentive alignment
- [ ] Regular feedback mechanisms

#### 6. Data Quality Risk
**Risk**: Incorrect/outdated data leads to poor recommendations
**Probability**: Low (weekly validation)
**Impact**: Suboptimal pricing decisions
**Mitigation**:
- [ ] Regular data quality checks
- [ ] Outlier detection and handling
- [ ] Data validation workflows
- [ ] Automated alerts for anomalies
- [ ] Manual review processes

### Safety Guardrails

#### Implemented Safeguards
1. **Price Change Limits**
   - Maximum single change: 20% per day
   - Maximum cumulative change: 50% per week
   - Requires approval for changes >15%

2. **Margin Protection**
   - Minimum margin enforcement: 20% (configurable)
   - Prevents unsustainable pricing
   - Alerts when margins compressed

3. **Inventory Limits**
   - Won't recommend aggressive discounts if inventory high
   - Won't enable stockouts through high pricing
   - Balances demand with availability

4. **Competitive Bounds**
   - Won't price 50% above market average
   - Won't price 50% below market average
   - Adjusts within competitive range

5. **Recommendation Review**
   - All recommendations reviewed before implementation
   - Dashboard shows justification
   - Override capability for business rules
   - Audit trail for all changes
