📊 Milestone 3 – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The objective of this milestone is to analyze the dataset to understand pricing behavior, demand patterns, and sales trends.
EDA was performed using visualizations such as price vs quantity, sales over time, revenue trends, and product-wise distribution.
The analysis helped identify demand patterns, price sensitivity (elasticity), and inventory impact on sales.
Key insights include variations in demand with price changes, seasonal sales trends, and identification of high-performing products.
These insights form the foundation for building an effective dynamic pricing model.
